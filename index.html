Sources you can check

Every āyah shows its sūrah and number. Every hadith shows its collection, number and grade, and we only include narrations graded ṣaḥīḥ or ḥasan. Where the schools of thought differ, the app tells you. We ask qualified scholars to review religious content before each release.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Do I need internet?

Only to open it the first time. After that Awwab works offline. Audio and Umrah maps are optional downloads.

Where does my data go?

Your prayers and progress stay on your device. You can export a backup file any time.

Which madhab does it follow?

Prayer examples start with the Hanafi structure. Choose how Asr is calculated in Settings. The guides note where scholars differ.

Who is Maktab mode for?

Anyone re-learning with simple lessons, bigger letters, and a takhti to write on.

Does it replace a teacher or a scholar?

Use Awwab alongside a teacher to practise. Ask a qualified scholar about religious rulings.

How do I install it?

Open Awwab in your browser. Look for Install app or Add to Home Screen in the browser menu.
