# H2 · Languages, learning paths & Arabic help

Three features, designed in Figma first (page **H2 · Languages, learning paths & Arabic help** [74:143](https://www.figma.com/design/cZzhFbOND8BtWNmryfFnIf/Prayer-Quest?node-id=74-143)):

1. **Language switching** — every English string in the app can switch to the main languages of Muslim-majority and large-Muslim-population countries that don't speak Arabic. → [01-languages.md](01-languages.md)
2. **Learning beyond the basics** — four paths (Read · Memorise · Understand · Speak) with five levels each: essential and advanced surahs, tajweed, Quranic grammar, and functional **spoken** Arabic. → [02-learning-paths.md](02-learning-paths.md)
3. **Ask in Arabic (Umrah mode)** — an offline phrase assistant that plays common requests aloud to people nearby (toilet, wudu, gate, SIM card, taxi, pharmacy, "I'm lost") and helps you understand the answer. → [03-umrah-arabic-assistant.md](03-umrah-arabic-assistant.md)

Seed data lives in [`data/`](data). Everything is draft content: it must pass native-speaker and scholar review before launch (see each doc).

## Implementation status

- Languages currently use the English and Bangla ICU catalogues. Preview builds also accept `?locale=en-XA` (expanded text) and `?locale=en-XB` (RTL) before the app’s hash route. The remaining locale packs, translation rights and reviewer sign-off are pending.
- Read Level 2 and Understand Level 4 use the offline Juz ʿAmma reader with separate saved progress. Learners can compare scripts, work through words and phrases, and open the attributed English translation. Read Level 5 adds full-surah recording, reference audio from installed packs or local imports, and a saved teacher-preparation checklist.
- Memorise Level 5 includes weekly self-assessed recital checks for all 30 juzs. The check hides the āyāt until requested, saves the next portion across sessions, and opens a week after completion. The daily Sabaq, Sabqi and Manzil reviews remain independent.
- Read Level 3 includes all eight Tajweed Essentials topics with 64 cited examples and progress on the Maktab map. Level 4 covers the 17 articulation points, breath and sound qualities, heavy letters, and a side-by-side IndoPak and Uthmani mark comparison. It has 40 cited drills, 36 with attributed offline recitations. Scholar sign-off and recordings for the remaining examples are pending.
- Understand includes ten Grammar Foundations units, four Sentence Structure lessons, and the full Roots & Patterns sequence. The roots lessons cover tense, commands, Forms I–X, participles, verbal nouns and negation with cited Quran examples and scheduled reviews. The Sentence Structure tafsīr lesson paraphrases an attributed Ibn Kathīr note.
- Speak includes twelve Saudi Hijazi A1 starter units, fourteen A2 Everyday units and twelve B1 Conversation units. A2 and B1 include conversations, script-based reply practice, signs, cited Quran root examples and offline vocabulary review. The 98 A2 clips and 120 B1 clips are generated with Piper. Rebuild a course with `python scripts/build-generated-speech.py <model.onnx> --course saudi-a2` or `--course saudi-b1`.
- Modern Standard Arabic A1 and Gulf Arabic A1 (Emirati) each include twelve lessons and 84 offline clips for dialogue, vocabulary and signs. Each variety saves its own lesson and vocabulary progress. Rebuild the MSA clips with `python scripts/build-generated-speech.py <nabra-82m-arabic-int8-dir> --course msa-a1` using `sherpa-onnx==1.13.8`, FFmpeg and the [Nabra INT8 model archive](https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx/releases/download/tts-models/nabra-82m-arabic-int8.tar.bz2). Rebuild the Gulf clips with the [Emirati Piper model and config](https://huggingface.co/vadimbelsky/arabic-emirati-female-piper/tree/cfe173969620e40be65b5dd8ed4d97d55a864325) and `--course gulf-a1`. Dialect, Bangla and generated pronunciation review are pending.
- Ask in Arabic offers a downloadable generated Opus pack for both text varieties, with one Emirati synthetic voice. The second voice and native pronunciation review remain pending. The pack can be rebuilt with `python scripts/build-generated-speech.py <model.onnx>` using `piper-tts`, the [voice model and config](https://huggingface.co/vadimbelsky/arabic-emirati-female-piper/tree/cfe173969620e40be65b5dd8ed4d97d55a864325), and FFmpeg. The Quran examples use cited recitations where available.
- Speak Level 4, Umrah & Hajj, includes nine offline lessons. It uses the same Arabic text, translations and 18 recordings as Ask in Arabic, with links between the two views. Rebuild the 45 additional clips for replies, vocabulary and signs with the Emirati Piper voice and `--course umrah-hajj`.
- Speak Level 5, Working in the Gulf, includes ten offline Emirati Arabic lessons. Topics cover applying for work, meeting colleagues, shifts, contracts, pay, leave, safety and asking about workplace rights. Rebuild the 70 audio clips with the Emirati Piper voice and `--course gulf-work`. Native review remains pending.

## Hard rules (unchanged)
No backend · fully offline after first load (language packs and audio packs are downloaded once and cached by the service worker) · tokens only · no container borders · equal-width siblings · every Arabic word opens Word anatomy · mercy-first copy · sources shown.

## Definition of done (whole handover)
- Switching language changes every visible string, number, date and Hijri month without reload; RTL locales mirror the shell; Arabic content components never mirror.
- Pseudo-locale (`en-XA`, +40% length) passes with no clipped text at 360px and 1280px.
- Paths: progress, SRS and placement work offline; every surah/passage shows its source and āyah range.
- Ask in Arabic: all phrases play offline from the pack; show mode stays awake (Wake Lock) at max brightness; helper answers translate into the app language.
- Native-speaker review signed off for each shipped locale and for the Saudi phrase set; scholar review for tajweed, passages' virtues (hadith grades) and glossary terms.
