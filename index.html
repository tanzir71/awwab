FAQ

Questions people ask

Do I need internet?

Open the app online first so it can prepare offline access. Map packs are optional downloads.

Where does my data go?

Your prayer logs, trip details and learning progress stay on your device. You can export a backup file at any time.

Which madhhab does it follow?

Prayer examples start with the Hanafi structure. Choose your Asr calculation in Settings. The guides note where scholars differ.

Who is Maktab mode for?

Anyone who wants to re-learn with short lessons, larger letters and a takhti. Children are welcome to learn with a parent.

Can I use it alongside a teacher?

Yes. Use Awwab to practise and remember. Check your recitation with a teacher and ask a scholar you trust for rulings.

How do I install it?

Open Awwab in your browser. Look for Install app or Add to Home Screen in the browser menu.
